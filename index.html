03

What is changing

the token economy

AI token subscriptions

Since May all three sell tokenised AI usage. China Telecom: three national tiers (consumers, developers and small businesses, ecosystem partners) bundled with TeleChat and DeepSeek. China Mobile: marketplace-style trials in several provinces. China Unicom: aggregator packages, starting in Shanghai.

Core under pressure

Weak consumer spending and a tax hike weigh on all three. China Mobile's Q3 profit fell 12.5%; the pivot is from "communications operator" to "technology services enterprise".

Network base

4.83M 5G base stations and 1.19B 5G subscribers nationally by Nov 2025 (65.3% of mobile). All three hold satellite mobile licences; MIIT is advancing 6G projects.

◈ The CEO line. The pipe is a mature utility. The growth case is selling compute and metered AI to subscribers who already pay every month. Try it in the Class Model tab.
